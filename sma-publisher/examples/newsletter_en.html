High-Dose Regimen of nusinersen receives European Commission Approval for the treatment of SMA
Biogen announced that the European Commission has granted marketing authorisation for a high-dose regimen of nusinersen (SPINRAZA®) for the treatment of SMA. The high-dose regimen comprises a more rapid loading phase, with two 50 mg loading doses administered 14 days apart, and 28 mg maintenance dose injections every four months thereafter.

Early nerve-muscle connection flaws set stage for SMA damage
A new review study published in Frontiers in Cellular Neuroscience found that in spinal muscular atrophy (SMA), the neuromuscular junction (NMJ) — where nerves connect to the muscles they control — fails to mature normally soon after birth, leaving it structurally underdeveloped and functionally vulnerable even before motor neurons start to die. This delayed maturation reduces neurotransmitter release, causes muscle weakness and quicker fatigue, and contributes to smaller electrical responses early in life. The researchers suggest that NMJ maturation parameters could serve as sensitive biomarkers for tracking SMA progression and assessing how well treatments are working.

Cure SMA Launches Phase 11 SMA Industry Collaboration
Cure SMA has launched Phase 11 of the SMA Industry Collaboration, a multi-faceted partnership with Novartis, Scholar Rock, Biogen, Genentech/Roche, argenx, NMD Pharma, and SMA Europe aimed at advancing SMA drug development and improving treatment access. The collaboration is organized into four Topic Groups covering clinical trial enablement, patient and caregiver-reported data, healthcare provider perspectives, and community education and engagement. Building on Phase 10 achievements — which included three peer-reviewed publications and the 9th Annual Community Update Survey — Phase 11 will focus on developing global patient-centered outcomes, distributing the 10th Annual Survey, and publishing the 2025 State of SMA Report.

EUPESMA 2023 Survey Results
This report is based on patient experience data from the pan-European EUPESMA survey, giving real insights into clinical trial participation and the community's experience and expectations for the future. Why it matters: Our goal is to make sure patient perspectives directly shape the design and planning of future clinical trials. Key highlights: Conducted in 51 countries and translated into 14 languages Counts on voices of people living with SMA, their parents, and caregivers Includes participants both with and without previous clinical trial experience This survey was carried out in collaboration with Tilburg University (Netherlands) and supported by the Cure SMA Industry Collaboration.
